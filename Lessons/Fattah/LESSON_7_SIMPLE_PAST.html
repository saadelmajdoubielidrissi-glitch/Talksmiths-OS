Step 1 — 🎬 Warm-Up (Conversation Starter)
My Memories
8 min
Objective: Share a memory or a simple past event from your life.
🎓 Student Strategy: To talk about the past, we use the simple past. Regular verbs end in **-ed** (paint → painted, work → worked). Irregular verbs change completely (go → went, write → wrote).
📋 Teacher Note: Use Baba's professional context. He retired from Barid Bank. Ask him: "When did you start working?" or "Where did you live when you were a child?" Let him bridge in Darija or French as he speaks.
🗣️ Speaking Warm-Up — Look back!

Think about yesterday. Which actions did you do?

  • 📖 Did you **read** a book?
  • 🎨 Did you **paint** a picture?
  • ☕ Did you **drink** green tea?
  • ⚽ Did you **watch** a football match?
Example: "Yesterday, I painted a beautiful tree and drank delicious tea."
🇲🇦 Darija Connection: In Darija we change the ending of the verb to show the past: *khdemt* (I worked), *ktebt* (I wrote). In English, we do the same!

📖 Past Tense Vocabulary & Bridges
Worked → خدم → A travaillé
Regular verb (work + ed).
"I worked at the bank for 30 years."
🇲🇦 Khdemt / 🇫🇷 J'ai travaillé
Painted → رسم → A peint
Regular verb (paint + ed).
"I painted a landscape of Mehdya beach."
🇲🇦 Rsemt / 🇫🇷 J'ai peint
Went (Irregular) → مشى → Est allé
The past of "Go". (Never say "goed"!).
"Last year, I went to Marrakech."
🇲🇦 Mshit / 🇫🇷 Je suis allé
Spoke (Irregular) → تكلم → A parlé
The past of "Speak".
"Yesterday, I spoke English with Saad."
🇲🇦 Hdert / 🇫🇷 J'ai parlé
Had (Irregular) → كان عندو → Avait
The past of "Have".
"Yesterday, I had an interesting lecture."
🇲🇦 Kan 3ndi / 🇫🇷 J'avais / J'ai eu
💡 Time Markers (Signaux du passé)
Words that indicate the past:
"Yesterday", "Last week", "Two years ago"
🇲🇦 Lbare7 / 🇫🇷 Hier / La semaine dernière

Step 3 — 🏗️ Grammar in Action
Regular vs Irregular Past
10 min
Objective: Correctly construct the past tense form of verbs.
📐 Spelling Rules for Regular Verbs:
Most verbs → Add -ed (walk → walked)
Verbs ending in -e → Add -d (live → lived)
Verbs ending in consonant + y → Change y to -ied (study → studied)
✏️ Write the past tense form of the verb in brackets:

1. (study) Yesterday, I English for one hour.

2. (go) My son to the market last weekend.

3. (paint) I a sunset of Mehdya beach last night.

4. (write) Saad a letter to me last Monday.

5. (have) We a wonderful tagine for lunch yesterday.
