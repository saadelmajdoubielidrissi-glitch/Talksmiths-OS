📖 Prepositions & Quantifiers Vocabulary

These small words tell us Where, When, and How Much.

In → ف (الداخل) → Dans / En
Used for enclosed spaces, months, years, and countries.
"In the box" — "In Morocco" — "In July"
🇲🇦 Ldakhl / F / 🇫🇷 Dans / En
On → فوق → Sur / Le
Used for surfaces and days of the week.
"On the table" — "On Sunday"
🇲🇦 Fou9 / 🇫🇷 Sur / (Le dimanche)
At → ف (المكان) → À
Used for specific points, events, and times.
"At the door" — "At 8:00 PM" — "At home"
🇲🇦 F / 🇫🇷 À
Some → شوية / بعض → Du / De la / Quelques
An unspecified quantity. Used in positive (+) sentences.
"I have some books." — "I want some water."
🇲🇦 Shwiya dial / 🇫🇷 Un peu de / Quelques
Any → حتى شي / واش كاين → De / Aucun(e)
Used in negative (-) sentences and questions (?).
"Do you have any books?" — "I don't have any water."
🇲🇦 7ta shi / Wesh kayn shi / 🇫🇷 Aucun(e) / De
Many → بزاف (يتحسب) → Beaucoup de
Used for things you can count (Plural nouns).
"I have many paintings." (1, 2, 3 paintings)
🇲🇦 Bezzaf dial (for countable) / 🇫🇷 Beaucoup de
Much → بزاف (مايتحسبش) → Beaucoup de
Used for things you CANNOT count (Uncountable nouns). Usually in negative/questions.
"I don't have much time." (You can't say "1 time, 2 times")
🇲🇦 Bezzaf dial (for uncountable) / 🇫🇷 Beaucoup de
💡 A lot of = Bezzaf!
"A lot of" is safe! It works for BOTH countable and uncountable in positive sentences!
"I have a lot of paintings" AND "I have a lot of time"
🇲🇦 The easiest way to say "Bezzaf" in English!

Step 3 — 🏗️ Grammar in Action
Time, Place, and Quantity
10 min
Objective: Practice using in, on, at, some, any, much, and many.
📐 The Preposition Triangle
IN (General/Big): Countries (In Morocco), Months (In May), Years (In 2026), Enclosed spaces (In the house)
ON (More Specific): Days (On Monday), Dates (On May 5th), Surfaces (On the table)
AT (Very Specific): Times (At 8:00), Specific locations (At school, At home)
📐 The Quantifier Rules
Some: Used in (+) positive sentences.
"I have some friends."
Any: Used in (-) negative and (?) questions.
"I don't have any money." "Do you have any questions?"
Many: For things you can count (1,2,3).
"Many books, many days."
Much: For things you CAN'T count.
"Much water, much time."
✏️ Fill in — IN, ON, or AT

1. The match is ___ Sunday ___ 8:00 PM.

2. Saad lives ___ Europe.

3. The painting is ___ the wall ___ the living room.

✏️ Fill in — SOME, ANY, MUCH, or MANY

1. Do you have ___ brothers?

2. I have ___ good news for you!

3. How ___ times did Morocco play in the World Cup?

4. We don't have ___ time left before the match starts.
