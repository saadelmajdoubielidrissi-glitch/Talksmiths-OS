📖 Everyday Expressions & Small Talk Phrases
How are you doing? / How is it going?
Common ways to ask how someone is.
"Hi Baba! How is it going?"
🇲🇦 Kidayr? / Labas? / 🇫🇷 Comment ça va?
Not bad / I am doing great
Positive and neutral responses.
"I am doing great, thank you."
🇲🇦 Labas / Bikhir / 🇫🇷 Pas mal / Ça va super
In my opinion... / I think...
Used to express personal views.
"In my opinion, Kenitra is a calm city."
🇲🇦 Fi nadari / 🇫🇷 À mon avis / Je pense
I need... / I want...
Expressing basic needs or desires.
"I need some water, please."
🇲🇦 Bghit / Khassni / 🇫🇷 J'ai besoin / Je veux
How is the weather?
Perfect conversational starter.
"How is the weather in Mehdya today?"
🇲🇦 Kidayr ljaw? / 🇫🇷 Quel temps fait-il?
💡 Simple Agreements
Showing you agree or understand:
"I agree." (🇫🇷 Je suis d'accord)
"Exactly!" (🇫🇷 Tout à fait)
🇲🇦 Nshan / Niyt / 🇫🇷 Tout à fait / D'accord

Step 3 — 🏗️ Expressions in Action
Expressing Opinions and Needs
10 min
Objective: Practice structuring opinions and expressing desires politely.
📐 Key Structures:
Opinion: **In my opinion,** + [sentence] (e.g. "In my opinion, tea is better.")
Desire: **I want to** + [action verb] (e.g. "I want to paint.")
Need: **I need** + [noun] (e.g. "I need fresh brush.")
✏️ Write the correct conversational connector:

1. (In my opinion / I want) , football is more exciting than tennis.

2. (I want to / I need) I am thirsty, so I drink a glass of water.

3. (I agree / How is) "I think Mehdya is beautiful." — ", it is spectacular!"

4. (Excuse me / How are you) " doing?" — "I am fine, thanks."

5. (I need / I want to) I have an anthropology exam, so I study tonight.
