📖 Core Greetings & Expressions
Nice to meet you → متشرفين → Enchanté
Said when meeting someone for the first time.
"Hello! I am Fattah. Nice to meet you."
🇲🇦 Mtsherfin / 🇫🇷 Enchanté de vous rencontrer
You're welcome → بلا جميل → De rien
The response to "Thank you".
"Thank you for the tea!" — "You're welcome, Baba."
🇲🇦 Bla jamil / La shukra 3la wajib / 🇫🇷 De rien
Excuse me → سمح لي → Excusez-moi
Polite way to catch someone's attention or pass.
"Excuse me, where is the library?"
🇲🇦 Sme7 lia / 🇫🇷 Excusez-moi / Pardon
Welcome → مرحبا → Bienvenue
To greet a guest arriving at a place.
"Welcome to Mehdya, my friend!"
🇲🇦 Merhba / 🇫🇷 Bienvenue
Have a nice day → يوم سعيد → Bonne journée
A friendly goodbye expression.
"Goodbye, Saad! Have a nice day!"
🇲🇦 Nharek mbarek / 🇫🇷 Bonne journée
💡 Please vs Thank you
The golden keys of English:
"Mint tea, please."
"Thank you very much!"
🇲🇦 3afak / Shukran bezaf / 🇫🇷 S'il vous plaît / Merci beaucoup

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
Welcoming a Foreign Professor
10 min
Objective: Read a conversation between Baba and a university speaker.
📋 Teacher Note: Read this dialogue together. Focus on tone: friendly, warm, and polite. Highlight the usage of "Excuse me" and "You're welcome".

Fattah: Excuse me, Professor. Welcome to Ibn Tofail University in Kenitra!

Professor: Good morning! Thank you very much. Nice to meet you!

Fattah: Nice to meet you too. I am Fattah, a student of anthropology here. Please, sit down.

Professor: That is very kind of you, Fattah. Thank you for the warm welcome.

Fattah: You're welcome. Have a wonderful day at our university!

📝 Check Your Understanding

1. How does Fattah greet the Professor at the beginning?

2. What subject does Baba Fattah study?

3. What does Fattah say when the Professor thanks him?

Step 3 — 🏗️ Expressions in Action
Choosing the Right Phrase
10 min
Objective: Match the polite phrase to the social context.
📐 The Conversation Guide:
If someone says **"Thank you"** → Answer **"You're welcome"**
If you need to pass through a crowd → Say **"Excuse me"**
If someone arrives at your hotel or city → Say **"Welcome!"**
✏️ Write the correct polite phrase (choose from: Excuse me, Thank you, Nice to meet you, You're welcome, Have a nice day):

1. "Here is your cup of green tea, Baba." — "!"

2. "Thank you for the delicious dinner!" — "."

3. (Before asking a stranger for directions): ", where is the beach?"

4. "This is my friend John." — "Hello John! ."

5. (When leaving a shop): "Goodbye! !"
