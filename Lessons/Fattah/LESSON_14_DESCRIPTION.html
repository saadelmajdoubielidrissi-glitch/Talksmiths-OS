Step 1 — 🎬 Warm-Up (Conversation Starter)
The People in Our Lives
8 min
Objective: Describe the looks and characteristics of family members and friends.
🎓 Student Strategy: We use **"to be"** for physical height or character qualities (He is tall, he is kind). We use **"to have"** for physical parts like hair and eyes (He has gray hair).
📋 Teacher Note: Prompt Baba to describe himself and his son Saad. Make sure he correctly separates "is" and "has" during the speaking phase.
🗣️ Speaking Warm-Up — Family Portraits

Describe these details using English:

  • 🧑 What does your son Saad look like? (Is he tall?)
  • 🎨 Describe your own character. (Are you quiet or active?)
  • 👓 Do you have gray hair and glasses?
Example: "My son Saad is tall and handsome. I am quiet and creative."
🇲🇦 Darija Connection: We say *Twil* (Tall), *Drayef* (Kind), and *3inih kahlin* (He has black eyes). Let's learn them in English!
🧠 Baba's Notebook

Describe yourself using 3 character words (like kind, hardworking, creative) and 2 appearance details.

📖 Physical Appearance & Personality Vocabulary
Tall / Short
Describing height. Used with "is".
"Saad is **tall**, but his younger cousin is **short**."
🇲🇦 Twil / Qsir / 🇫🇷 Grand / Petit
Gray hair / Brown eyes
Physical details. Used with "has/have".
"I have **gray hair** and **brown eyes**."
🇲🇦 Sh3r khrif / 3inin qehwyin / 🇫🇷 Cheveux gris / Yeux marrons
Kind / Friendly
Warm personality qualities.
"The banker was always **kind** and **friendly** to customers."
🇲🇦 Drayef / Sadiqi / 🇫🇷 Gentil / Sympathique
Creative / Hardworking
Active, productive qualities.
"A painter must be very **creative**."
🇲🇦 Mobdi3 / Khdam / 🇫🇷 Créatif / Travailleur
Quiet / Intelligent
Calm, intellectual qualities.
"My art room is **quiet** and study is interesting."
🇲🇦 Hadi / Daki / 🇫🇷 Calme / Intelligent
💡 Grammar Rule: "IS" vs "HAS"
The description split:
He **is** [Adjective] (e.g. intelligent)
He **has** [Noun] (e.g. gray beard)
🇫🇷 Il est (intelligent) vs Il a (une barbe grise)

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
Saad's Portrait of His Father
10 min
Objective: Read a descriptive text about Baba Fattah's appearance and traits.
📋 Teacher Note: Have him read the text. It describes him perfectly. Ask him: "Do you agree with Saad's description?"

My father, Fattah, **is** a retired bank director. He **is** sixty-two years old, but he is very **active** and **healthy**. He **has** gray hair, a kind smile, and brown eyes.

He **is** a very **creative** painter. In his studio, he works **peacefully** for hours. He **is** also a **hardworking** anthropology student at Ibn Tofail University. Everyone thinks he **is** extremely **intelligent** and **quiet**.

He **is** **friendly** to his university friends and loves to drink sweet mint tea with them. He is the best father in the world!

📝 Check Your Understanding

1. What physical appearance details does Fattah have?

2. Name three personality traits of Baba Fattah mentioned in the text.

3. What makes him a friendly host?

Step 3 — 🏗️ Vocabulary in Action
Choosing "Is" or "Has"
10 min
Objective: Correctly apply the physical descriptive verbs in sentences.
📐 Decision Guide:
Use **IS** for height, age, and character adjectives (e.g. He is kind, he is old).
Use **HAS** for hair, eyes, beard, or clothes nouns (e.g. He has a blue shirt).
✏️ Write: "is" or "has"

1. Saad very tall and friendly.

2. My father gray hair and brown eyes.

3. A creative painter very imaginative.

4. Ibn Tofail University many intelligent students.

5. He a kind smile on his face.

📋 Lesson 14 Summary — What You Learned Today
✅ **IS** → Used for age, height, and personality (e.g. He is hardworking)
✅ **HAS** → Used for physical features (e.g. He has gray beard)
✅ **Personality words** → kind, hardworking, creative, intelligent, quiet
✅ **Appearance words** → tall, short, gray hair, brown eyes
