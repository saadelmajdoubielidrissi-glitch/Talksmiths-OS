The Curious Student

Objective: Learn how to ask for information. You are a student of Anthropology — asking questions is your superpower!
🎓 Student Strategy: Don't try to translate the whole sentence from Darija. Start by learning just the FIRST word of the question.

📖 The 6 Important Question Words

There are 6 main question words in English. 5 start with "W" and 1 starts with "H".

What → أشنو → Quoi / Que
Asking for information about a thing or action.
"What is this?" — "What is your job?"
🇲🇦 Ash / Ashnou / 🇫🇷 Quoi / Quel
Where → فين → Où
Asking about a place or location.
"Where is my phone?" — "Where do you live?"
🇲🇦 Fin / 🇫🇷 Où
Who → شكون → Qui
Asking about a person.
"Who is she?" — "Who won the match?"
🇲🇦 Shkoun / 🇫🇷 Qui
When → إمتى → Quand
Asking about time or dates.
"When is the game?" — "When are you coming?"
🇲🇦 Imta / Fou9ash / 🇫🇷 Quand
Why → علاش → Pourquoi
Asking for a reason. (Answer usually starts with "Because").
"Why are you happy?" — "Why is he late?"
🇲🇦 3lash / 🇫🇷 Pourquoi
How → كيفاش → Comment
Asking about the way something happens, or someone's condition.
"How are you?" — "How do you paint this?"
🇲🇦 Kifash / 🇫🇷 Comment
💡 How + Old / Much / Many
"How" can combine with other words!
How old are you? (Age)
How much is this? (Price/Quantity)
🇲🇦 Sh7al f 3emrek? / Sh7al taman?
💡 Because = Parce que / 7it
When someone asks Why, you answer with Because.
"Why are you tired?" → "Because I studied all night."
🇫🇷 Pourquoi → Parce que

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
The Anthropology Student Interview
10 min
Objective: Read an interview and notice how questions and answers fit together.
📋 Teacher Note: This is a dialogue. You read the Interviewer (Saad), and Baba reads "Fattah". This is great speaking practice. Focus on the intonation of questions (voice goes down at the end of WH questions in English, unlike yes/no questions!).
🗣️ Interviewer:
Hello sir! What is your name?
🗣️ Fattah:
My name is Fattah.
🗣️ Interviewer:
Where are you from?
🗣️ Fattah:
I am from Morocco. I live in Mehdya.
🗣️ Interviewer:
I see you have many books. What do you study?
🗣️ Fattah:
I study Anthropology at the university.
🗣️ Interviewer:
That is very interesting. Why do you study Anthropology?
🗣️ Fattah:
Because I want to understand human culture and society.
🗣️ Interviewer:
When are your classes?
🗣️ Fattah:
My classes are in the afternoon.
🗣️ Interviewer:
Who helps you practice English for your studies?
🗣️ Fattah:
My son Saad helps me. He is a teacher.
🗣️ Interviewer:
How is your English now?
🗣️ Fattah:
It is getting better every day!

Look at the interview. Write the answer Fattah gives for each question word:

Why? (Reason)
Who? (Person)

Step 3 — 🏗️ Grammar in Action
The Question Formula
10 min
Objective: Learn the correct word order for making questions.
📋 Teacher Note: In French, we can just raise our voice: "Tu es marocain ?". In English, we must INVERT the verb and the subject. This is the biggest hurdle. Focus heavily on: "You are" -> "Are you?". "He is" -> "Is he?".
📐 The Magic Question Formula
WH-Word + Verb (to be) + Subject ?
Where
are
you?
Who
is
he?
What
is
that?
🇫🇷 French Parallel: English questions use "inversion" just like formal French:
"Tu es Fattah." → "Es-tu Fattah ?"
"You are Fattah." → "Are you Fattah?"
✏️ Unscramble the Questions

Put the words in the correct order to make a question.

1. (are / where / you / ?)

2. (is / who / she / ?)

3. (your name / what / is / ?)

4. (the match / when / is / ?)

5. (happy / why / are / they / ?)
