📖 Articles — The Three Little Words That Change Everything

English has 3 articles: a, an, and the. Just like un, une, le, la, les in French — but SIMPLER!

A → واحد → Un / Une
Used before consonant sounds for any one thing (not specific)
"I have a book." — "She is a teacher."
🇲🇦 Wa7ed l-ktab / 🇫🇷 Un livre
An → واحد → Un / Une
Same as "a" but before vowel sounds (a, e, i, o, u)
"He is an artist." — "I eat an orange."
🇲🇦 Same meaning as "a"! / 🇫🇷 Un artiste, une orange
The → ل- → Le / La / Les
Used for one specific thing (both speaker and listener know which one)
"The painting on the wall is beautiful."
🇲🇦 L-ktab (the book), L-bab (the door) / 🇫🇷 Le livre, la porte
No Article → Ø → (varies)
Used with plural general things and uncountable nouns
"I love Ø football." — "Ø Water is important."
🇲🇦 Kanbghi l-kora / 🇫🇷 J'aime LE football — English drops it!
🔑 The "A vs AN" Rule
It's about SOUND, not the letter!
A university (starts with "yoo" sound) ✅
An hour (the H is silent!) ✅
🇫🇷 Like French liaison! It's about flow.
🔑 The "A vs THE" Rule
A = any one (first time). The = this specific one (we both know)
"I see a cat." (any cat)
"The cat is on the roof." (THAT specific cat)
🇫🇷 Same as un chat → le chat. Exactement pareil!
Singular Countable
MUST have an article: a/an or the
"I need a pen." ✅ "I need pen." ❌
🇲🇦 In Darija "bghit stylo" works, in English you NEED "a"!
Proper Nouns
Names of people, cities, countries — usually NO article
"Ø Fattah lives in Ø Morocco." ✅
BUT: "The USA", "The Atlantic Ocean" 🤷
🇫🇷 Like French! "Fattah habite au Maroc" — no article on Fattah!

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
A Day at the Beach
10 min
Objective: Read a short story and notice how articles (a/an/the) are used naturally.
📋 Teacher Note: Read aloud together. Every time you encounter an article, pause and ask "Why 'a' here? Why 'the' there?" Help him see the pattern: first mention = a/an, second mention or specific = the. The story is set in Mehdya — his own neighbourhood — making it personal and relatable.

Today is a beautiful day. I take a walk to the beach near my house in Mehdya. The sun is shining and the sky is blue.

I see a man with a fishing rod. The man is sitting on a rock near the water. He has a bucket next to him.

I walk to a small café by the beach. I order a coffee and an orange juice. The coffee is strong and delicious — just like Ø Moroccan coffee!

I sit and look at the ocean. The waves are calm today. A seagull flies over my head. The seagull lands on the sand. It is a peaceful morning.

After an hour, I walk back home through the forest. I love Ø nature. It makes me happy.

📝 Comprehension — Answer in English

1. Where does the story take place?

2. What does the man on the rock have?

3. What does Fattah order at the café?

🔍 Article Detective — Why "A" or "THE"?

Look at these pairs from the story. Why does the article change?

1. "I see a man" → then "The man is sitting..." — Why?

2. "I order a coffee" → "The coffee is strong" — Why?

3. Why "I love Ø nature" with NO article?

Step 3 — 🏗️ Grammar in Action
The Rules of A, An & The
10 min
Objective: Master the 4 key rules for using articles correctly.
📋 Teacher Note: Don't overwhelm with rules. Focus on the BIG TWO: 1) a/an = general/first time, 2) the = specific/known. The "no article" rule for general plurals/uncountables can be introduced gently. Use French as the constant bridge — it maps almost perfectly.
📐 Rule 1 — Use "A / AN" when...
✅ Talking about something for the FIRST TIME
✅ Talking about ANY ONE of something (not specific)
✅ Describing someone's job: "She is a teacher."
🇫🇷 Exactly like un/une in French! "Elle est une professeure."
📐 Rule 2 — Use "THE" when...
✅ Both speaker and listener know WHICH ONE
✅ There is ONLY ONE: "the sun", "the moon", "the president"
✅ You already MENTIONED IT before: "a cat" → "the cat"
🇫🇷 Exactly like le/la/les! / 🇲🇦 Like the "l-" prefix: l-bab, sh-shems
📐 Rule 3 — Use NO ARTICLE when...
✅ General plurals: "I love Ø cats." (cats in general)
✅ Uncountable nouns in general: "Ø Water is life."
✅ Names, cities, countries: "Ø Morocco is beautiful."
⚠️ CAREFUL: French uses articles here! "J'aime les chats" BUT English: "I love cats" (no "the"!)
✏️ Fill in — A, AN, THE, or Ø (nothing)

1. I live in ___ beautiful house near ___ beach.

2. ___ sun is very hot today.

3. Lamia is ___ headmistress. She loves ___ education.

4. I want to buy ___ new painting for ___ living room.

5. ___ Morocco played ___ amazing match yesterday.

6. He is ___ engineer. He studies at ___ ENSA.

7. I eat ___ apple every morning. ___ apples are healthy.

8. ___ Atlantic Ocean is near ___ Kenitra.
