📖 Comparative Vocabulary & Rules
Taller / Smaller
Used for short adjectives (+ -er).
"I am taller than my brother."
🇫🇷 Plus grand / plus petit
More beautiful / More interesting
Used for long adjectives (more + word).
"Painting is more interesting than watching TV."
🇫🇷 Plus beau / plus intéressant
Better (Irregular)
The comparative of "good". Never say "gooder"!
"Moroccan tagine is better than fast food."
🇲🇦 7sen men / 🇫🇷 Meilleur que
Worse (Irregular)
The comparative of "bad". Never say "badder"!
"Rain is bad, but a storm is worse than rain."
🇲🇦 Kفسد من / 🇫🇷 Pire que
As... as → بحال... بحال
Shows that two things are equal (=).
"Mehdya is as warm as Rabat today."
🇲🇦 B7al b7al / 🇫🇷 Aussi... que
💡 The Golden Rule: "THAN"
We always use **than** (que) after the comparative!
"Morocco is warmer than France." (NOT "tahat" or "that")
🇲🇦 Men / 🇫🇷 Que

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
The Art Studio vs The Lecture Hall
10 min
Objective: Read a short dialogue comparing two favorite hobbies.
📋 Teacher Note: Have Fattah read both roles (or play them together). Focus on the pronunciation of "-er" (tall-er, loud-er) and the word "than".

Fattah: I love painting because my studio is quiet. It is quieter than the university lecture hall.

Saad: That is true, Baba. But at the university, you meet many students. Isn't that more exciting than sitting alone?

Fattah: Yes, it is more exciting, but it is also more tiring! Painting makes me feel relaxed. The beach in Mehdya is better than a busy classroom.

Saad: Haha, I agree! Mehdya's sea air is fresher than anything else!

📝 Comprehension & Logic Check

1. Why does Fattah prefer his art studio over the university lecture hall?

2. What does Saad think is more exciting than sitting alone?

3. What does Fattah say about the sea air in Mehdya?

Step 3 — 🏗️ Grammar in Action
Building Comparisons
10 min
Objective: Practice modifying adjectives into comparative forms.
📐 How to build them:
Short words (1 syllable): Add -er (fast → faster)
Short words ending in -y: Change y to -ier (busy → busier)
Long words (2+ syllables): Use more (famous → more famous)
✏️ Write the correct comparative form (e.g. "taller than", "more expensive than")

1. (big) A house is an apartment.

2. (easy) English is Chinese.

3. (expensive) A new car is a bicycle.

4. (good) Painting with oil is drawing with a pencil.

5. (hot) Marrakech is Kenitra.
