Step 1 — 🎬 Warm-Up (Conversation Starter)
The Magic Glue
8 min
Objective: Learn how to connect two short sentences into one long sentence.
🎓 Student Strategy: Short sentences sound robotic. "I like tea. I like coffee." Connectors make you sound like a native speaker: "I like tea AND coffee."
Saad (teachers instructions)
Baba, connectors like 'but', 'so', and 'because' are the 'glue' of English! They help you connect short sentences to make your English sound smooth and beautiful. Let's see how they work!
🗣️ Speaking Warm-Up — Combine the Sentences

What word can we put in the middle?

"I am tired." ➕ "I want to sleep."
→ I am tired, I want to sleep.
"I love Morocco." ➕ "I don't like the heat."
→ I love Morocco, I don't like the heat.
🇲🇦 Darija Connection: In Darija we say "Ou" (And), "Walakin" (But), "7it" (Because). Today we learn their English twins!
🧠 Baba's Notebook

Write down the 5 main connectors. They are short but incredibly powerful.

  • AND = __________
  • BUT = __________
  • BECAUSE = __________
  • SO = __________
📖 The 5 Essential Connectors
And → و → Et
Adds information (+).
"I like reading and I like painting."
🇲🇦 Ou / 🇫🇷 Et
But → ولكن → Mais
Shows a difference or contrast (-).
"I like tea, but I don't like coffee."
🇲🇦 Walakin / 🇫🇷 Mais
Because → حيت / لحقاش → Parce que
Gives a reason (Why).
"I am happy because my son is here."
🇲🇦 7it / La7qash / 🇫🇷 Parce que / Car
So → داكشي علاش → Donc
Shows a result (The consequence).
"It is raining, so I will stay at home."
🇲🇦 Dakshi 3lash / 🇫🇷 Donc / Alors
Or → ولا / أو → Ou / Ou bien
Gives a choice (Option A or Option B).
"Do you want tea or coffee?"
🇲🇦 Wla / 🇫🇷 Ou
💡 Because vs So
They are opposites! Reason vs Result.
"I sleep because I am tired."
"I am tired, so I sleep."
🇫🇷 Parce que vs Donc

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
A Letter About Anthropology
10 min
Objective: Read an email and identify the connectors.
Saad (teachers instructions)
Here is a letter from you to me! Read it out loud, and whenever you see a bold connector, try to say what it means in Darija (like 'walakin' for 'but', or '7it' for 'because'). It's a great bridge!

Subject: My Studies at the University 🎓

Hello my friend,

I am writing to you because I have exciting news. I am studying Anthropology at the university now! I read many books every day and I go to lectures in the afternoon.

It is very interesting, but it is also difficult. The books are in French, so I have to focus a lot. Sometimes I study in the library, or I study at home in the garden.

I am very busy, so I don't paint as much as before. But I am very happy because I am learning new things every day.

Best regards,
Fattah

📝 Check Your Understanding

1. Why is Fattah writing the letter?

2. Why does he have to focus a lot?

3. What is the result of him being very busy?

Step 3 — 🏗️ Grammar in Action
Linking Ideas
10 min
Objective: Practice choosing the correct connector for the sentence.
📐 The Logic Test
If the idea is a SURPRISE or OPPOSITE → Use BUT
If the idea is a REASON (Why?) → Use BECAUSE
If the idea is a RESULT (Action) → Use SO
🇫🇷 French Check:
Il pleut, donc je reste. = It is raining, so I stay.
Je reste parce qu'il pleut. = I stay because it is raining.
✏️ Choose: AND, BUT, BECAUSE, or SO

1. I like football, I don't like basketball.

2. I am hungry, I will eat an apple.

3. I will eat an apple I am hungry.

4. I have a pen a notebook.

5. Do you want to go to the beach the forest?

Step 5 — 🗣️ Speaking Challenge
My Weekend Plans
10 min
Objective: Speak for 1 minute using AND, BUT, BECAUSE, and SO.
Saad (teachers instructions)
Tell me: what are your plans for this weekend? As you speak, I'll challenge you by giving you words like 'but...', 'so...', or 'because...' to help you expand your sentences. Let's keep the flow going!

🎤 Weekend Story Challenge

Tell me about your plans for Saturday. You MUST use:

  • ✅ And (to add an activity)
  • ✅ But (to mention a problem)
  • ✅ Because (to give a reason)
  • ✅ So (what you will do instead)
📝 Example to read aloud
"On Saturday, I want to go to the beach AND swim. BUT the weather is cold, SO I will stay home. I will paint BECAUSE I love art."
Now write your own ideas!

📋 Lesson 5 Summary — What You Learned Today
✅ AND = Add things together (+)
✅ BUT = Show a difference or problem (-)
✅ BECAUSE = Give a reason (Why?)
✅ SO = Give a result (Action!)
✅ OR = Make a choice (A or B)
