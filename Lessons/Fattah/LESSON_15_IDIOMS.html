📖 6 Essential Daily Life Idioms
A piece of cake
Something that is extremely easy to do.
"Don't worry about the exam, Baba. It's a piece of cake!"
🇲🇦 Sahl mahel / 🇫🇷 C'est du gâteau
Under the weather
Feeling slightly sick, tired, or unwell.
"I cannot walk by the sea today because I am under the weather."
🇲🇦 3yan shwia / 🇫🇷 Être un peu patraque / malade
Cost an arm and a leg
Extremely expensive.
"This imported oil paint costs an arm and a leg!"
🇲🇦 Ghali bezaf / Tayyeb l-jib / 🇫🇷 Coûter les yeux de la tête
Hold your horses
Slow down, be patient, wait a moment.
"Hold your horses, Baba! Let's drink our tea first before studying."
🇲🇦 Sbbar shwia / T9el / 🇫🇷 Minute papillon / Attends un peu
Spill the beans
To reveal a secret or show the surprise early.
"Come on, Saad! Spill the beans. What is my birthday surprise?"
🇲🇦 Khwi l-kar / Tle9 s-sarout / 🇫🇷 Vendre la mèche
💡 Once in a blue moon
Happening very rarely.
"I eat fast food only once in a blue moon."
🇲🇦 Mra fi zman / 🇫🇷 Tous les trente-six du mois

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
The Art Supplies Shock
10 min
Objective: Read a natural dialogue using daily idioms and check comprehension.
Saad (Your Teacher)
Encourage Baba to read the dialogue. Help him identify the idioms "cost an arm and a leg", "under the weather", and "a piece of cake" in context.

Fattah: Hello Saad. Yesterday, I went to the art shop in Rabat to buy new brushes. They **cost an arm and a leg**!

Saad: Oh no! Why are they so expensive? Did you buy them?

Fattah: Yes, I had to. But I felt a bit **under the weather** afterwards because my wallet was empty!

Saad: Haha, don't worry, Baba. Tomorrow you will paint a masterpiece. Learning to paint is difficult, but learning to spend money is **a piece of cake**!

Fattah: That is very true. I will start painting tomorrow morning, Insha'Allah.

📝 Check Your Understanding

1. What did the new paint brushes cost?

2. How did Fattah feel after visiting the art shop?

3. What does Saad say is "a piece of cake"?

Step 3 — 🏗️ Idioms in Action
Decoding the Idiom Context
10 min
Objective: Practice choosing the correct idiom for each daily situation.
📐 The Translation Keys:
If an activity is extremely easy → Use **"a piece of cake"**
If you need someone to wait or be patient → Say **"hold your horses"**
If someone is feeling slightly sick or tired → Use **"under the weather"**
