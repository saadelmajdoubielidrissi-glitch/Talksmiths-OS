Step 1 — 🎬 Warm-Up (Conversation Starter)
How Do You Live?
8 min
Objective: Learn how to describe *how often* and *how* you do activities.
🎓 Student Strategy: Adverbs tell us more about actions. "I paint **carefully**." "I **always** drink tea." They add flavor to your sentences!
Saad (Your Teacher)
Differentiate between adverbs of frequency (always, often) and adverbs of manner (carefully, slowly). Focus on their positions in a sentence.
🗣️ Speaking Warm-Up — How often do you...?

Answer these simple questions using **Always, Often, Sometimes, or Never**:

  • 🎨 How often do you paint?
  • 🌊 How often do you walk by the sea in Mehdya?
  • ☕ How often do you drink sweet Moroccan tea?
Example: "I always drink tea in the afternoon, and I sometimes paint in the evening."
🇲🇦 Darija Connection: In Darija we say "Dima" (Always), "B3d lmerrat" (Sometimes), or "Bshwia" (Slowly). Today we learn their English matches!
🧠 Baba's Notebook

Write down your adverbs. List 3 things you **always** do and 1 thing you **never** do.

  • I always __________ in the morning.
  • I never __________ during my lectures.
📖 Key Adverbs & Word Order Rules
Always → ديما → Toujours
100% of the time. Goes BEFORE the main verb.
"I always wake up at 7:00 AM."
🇲🇦 Dima / 🇫🇷 Toujours
Often / Sometimes
70% / 50% of the time.
"I often walk on Mehdya beach."
🇲🇦 Ghaleban / B3d lmerrat / 🇫🇷 Souvent / Parfois
Never → عمرني → Jamais
0% of the time. (Use with positive verbs!).
"I never drink black coffee."
🇲🇦 3mmri / 🇫🇷 Jamais
Carefully → بتركيز / بشويا → Attentivement
Adverb of manner (tells HOW). Add -ly to adjectives.
"Fattah paints carefully."
🇲🇦 B-tartib / 🇫🇷 Avec soin / Soigneusement
Slowly / Quickly
Describes speed of action.
"I walk slowly, but I think quickly."
🇲🇦 B-shwia / Dghya / 🇫🇷 Lentement / Rapidement
💡 Word Order Rule
Frequency adverbs go BEFORE standard verbs, but AFTER "to be"!
"I always paint." vs "I am always happy."
🇫🇷 Avant le verbe / Après le verbe "être"

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
Fattah's Peaceful Day
10 min
Objective: Read a short description of Baba's routine and find adverbs.
Saad (Your Teacher)
Have him read the text. Encourage him to notice that "peacefully", "carefully", and "slowly" end in "-ly" and describe the verb.

I **always** start my day with a warm cup of mint tea. In the morning, I sit **peacefully** in my garden. I **usually** sketch a new idea for a painting.

In the afternoon, I walk **slowly** along Mehdya beach. The sea air is refreshing. I **sometimes** meet friends at a local cafe, and we talk **happily** about art and history.

In the evening, I **never** watch loud television. I prefer to read my anthropology books **quietly** before sleeping.

📝 Comprehension & Adverb Search

1. How does Fattah start his day?

2. How does he walk along the beach?

3. What does he never do in the evening?

Step 3 — 🏗️ Grammar in Action
Building Adverbs of Manner
10 min
Objective: Practice changing adjectives to adverbs of manner.
📐 Adjective vs Adverb:
Adjectives describe **things** (nouns): "A **quiet** room."
Adverbs describe **actions** (verbs): "He reads **quietly**."
To build: Adjective + **-ly** (careful → carefully)
✏️ Complete the sentences with the adverb form of the word in brackets:

1. (careful) I paint the details .

2. (quick) He runs to catch the bus.

3. (happy) The father and son spoke about their plans.

4. (slow) The old banker walked down the street.

5. (quiet) We must study in the university library.
