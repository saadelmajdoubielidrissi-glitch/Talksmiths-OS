Step 1 — 🎬 Warm-Up (Conversation Starter)
My Tomorrow & My Next Year
8 min
Objective: Learn the difference between sudden decisions and fixed plans.
🎓 Student Strategy: In English, we use two structures for the future. We use **Going to** for fixed plans we made in advance. We use **Will** for quick decisions or guesses.
📋 Teacher Note: Use standard examples to show the gap. "I am going to visit Saad in Hungary" (plan already made, flights booked) vs. "I think the weather will be hot tomorrow" (guess/prediction).

📖 Future Forms: Will vs Going to
Going to (Plans)
Subject + to be + going to + verb. Used for decisions made BEFORE speaking.
"I am going to study history next semester."
🇲🇦 Ghadin... / 🇫🇷 Aller + verbe (Je vais étudier)
Will (Quick Decisions)
Subject + will + verb. Used for decisions made AT the moment of speaking.
"The phone is ringing. I will answer it!"
🇲🇦 Gha-... / 🇫🇷 Futur simple (Je répondrai)
Will (Predictions)
Used for predictions or guesses about the future.
"I think it will rain tomorrow in Kenitra."
🇫🇷 Je pense qu'il pleuvra
Won't (Will Not)
The negative form of "will".
"Don't worry, I won't forget my keys."
🇲🇦 Ma-ghadin-sh / 🇫🇷 Je n'oublierai pas
Not going to
The negative form of "going to".
"I am not going to watch the movie tonight."
🇲🇦 Ma-ghadi-sh / 🇫🇷 Je ne vais pas regarder
💡 Decision Test
Did you prepare the plan?
YES → Use Going to.
NO (Instant/Guess) → Use Will.
🇫🇷 Projet vs Décision instantanée

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
Saad's Visit & Future Exhibitions
10 min
Objective: Read a conversation about future projects and identify plans vs guesses.
📋 Teacher Note: Have him read the dialogue aloud. Point out that "am going to travel" and "are going to host" are fixed plans, while "will love" is a prediction.

Fattah: I have exciting news! Next month, I **am going to travel** to Budapest to visit you, Saad!

Saad: Excellent, Baba! We **are going to explore** the historical sights of Hungary together. I think you **will love** the architecture!

Fattah: Yes, I am very excited. I **will buy** some local art supplies there, too. When I return, I **am going to paint** a large canvas about my journey.

Saad: That sounds wonderful. I am sure it **will be** a masterpiece!

📝 Comprehension & Logic Check

1. Where is Fattah going to travel next month?

2. What does Saad predict about Fattah's reaction?

3. What is Fattah going to do when he returns to Morocco?

Step 3 — 🏗️ Grammar in Action
Building Future Sentences
10 min
Objective: Practice choosing between Will and Going to.
📐 Quick Test:
If there is **evidence** now (e.g. black clouds, a plane ticket) → Use **Going to**
If it is a **promise** or **quick offer** (e.g. "I will help you") → Use **Will**
✏️ Write: "will" or "going to" (with "am/is/are" where needed)

1. I have my ticket. I visit Saad next month.

2. (The phone rings) Don't stand up. I answer it.

3. Look at those dark clouds! It rain.

4. I think Morocco win the match tonight.

5. I bought some canvas. I paint a sunset tomorrow.
