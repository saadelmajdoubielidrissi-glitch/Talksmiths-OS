Step 1 — 🎬 Warm-Up (Conversation Starter)
The Seaside Climate
8 min
Objective: Learn to describe Mehdya's weather across the four seasons.
🎓 Student Strategy: The weather is a universal topic. We use structures like **"It is sunny"** or **"It is raining"** to describe daily conditions.
📋 Teacher Note: Use Mehdya's seaside weather to prompt comparisons. Winter (rainy, cold, windy) vs. Summer (hot, sunny, perfect for swimming).
🗣️ Speaking Warm-Up — Look Out the Window

Describe Mehdya's weather today:

  • ☀️ Is the sun shining? (**It is sunny**)
  • 💨 Is there wind from the ocean? (**It is windy**)
  • 🌊 Which season is your favorite for walking by the sea?
Example: "Today it is sunny and warm. My favorite season is spring."
🇲🇦 Darija Connection: We say *Rbi3* (Spring), *Chta* (Rain), and *Skhoun* (Hot). Today we learn how to say them in English!
🧠 Baba's Notebook

Write down the four seasons in English and describe the weather of Kenitra in January and July.

📖 Core Vocabulary: Seasons & Weather
Spring / Summer
Warm, sunny seasons. Perfect for painting outdoors.
"Mehdya beach is full of visitors in **summer**."
🇲🇦 Rbi3, Sif / 🇫🇷 Printemps, Été
Autumn / Winter
Cooler, rainy seasons.
"In **winter**, the ocean wind is very cold."
🇲🇦 Khrif, Chta / 🇫🇷 Automne, Hiver
Sunny / Rainy
Describing the sky (sun vs rain).
"It is **sunny** today, but yesterday it was **rainy**."
🇲🇦 Mshemesh / Kati7 chta / 🇫🇷 Ensoleillé / Pluvieux
Windy / Cloudy
Wind or clouds present.
"The coastal air is **windy** in the evening."
🇲🇦 Fiha lberd / Mghiem / 🇫🇷 Venteux / Nuageux
Hot / Cold
Describing temperature.
"Marrakech is **hot**, but Mehdya is fresher."
🇲🇦 Skhoun / Bared / 🇫🇷 Chaud / Froid
💡 Grammar Rule: "IT IS..."
Always start weather descriptions with **It is**:
"It is warm." (Not "Is warm" or "The weather are warm")
🇫🇷 Il fait... (Il fait chaud)

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
The Four Lights of Mehdya
10 min
Objective: Read about how seasons affect a painter's light and find weather terms.
📋 Teacher Note: Have him read the text. Point out how the seasons change his painting light. Contrast "sunny" and "cloudy".

As a painter, I love all the seasons. In **spring**, the sky is clear and the weather is warm. The light is perfect for mixing watercolors in my garden.

In **summer**, Mehdya is very hot and **sunny**. I sit under my umbrella on the beach and paint the bright blue sea.

In **autumn**, the air becomes **windy** and cool. The leaves fall **slowly** from the trees. In **winter**, the weather is **cold** and **rainy**. I stay inside my cozy home, drink hot green tea, and read my university books.

📝 Check Your Understanding

1. Why does Fattah love spring for painting?

2. What is the weather like on the beach in summer?

3. What does he do when the weather is rainy and cold in winter?

Step 3 — 🏗️ Vocabulary in Action
Describing Current Weather
10 min
Objective: Practice using "It is" + weather adjectives.
📐 Adjective structure:
Structure: **It is** + [weather adjective] (e.g. "It is rainy today.")
Action: **It is** + [verb-ing] (e.g. "It is raining now.")
✏️ Fill in the blanks with the correct weather adjective:

1. Put on your jacket! It is very outside.

2. You need an umbrella today because it is .

3. The sun is shining brightly. It is and warm.

4. The wind is blowing the sea sand. It is very today.

5. I cannot see the horizon because the sky is and dark.
