Step 1 — 🎬 Warm-Up (Conversation Starter)
The Palette & The Calendar
8 min
Objective: Learn how to name colors on your palette and read dates on a calendar.
🎓 Student Strategy: Numbers and colors are the building blocks of descriptions. You use them when discussing prices, your age, history lectures, and your oil paintings!
📋 Teacher Note: Connect colors to his painting palette (e.g. ultramarine blue, cadmium red) and numbers to historical years (like 1960 or 2026).
🗣️ Speaking Warm-Up — Look and Describe

Describe these details using English:

  • 🎨 What is your favorite **color** to paint with?
  • 📅 What **day of the week** is today?
  • 🔢 What **year** were you born? (e.g. nineteen sixty)
Example: "My favorite color is blue, today is Thursday, and my birth year is nineteen sixty-five."
🇲🇦 Darija Connection: In Darija we say *Zreg* (Blue), *Khmes* (Thursday), and *Tlatin* (Thirty). Let's learn them in English!

📖 Core Vocabulary: Colors, Days & Years
Red, Blue, Green, Yellow
Primary and natural colors.
"The Mehdya sea is **blue** and the trees are **green**."
🇲🇦 Ahmer, Zreg, Khder, Sfer / 🇫🇷 Rouge, Bleu, Vert, Jaune
White, Black, Brown
Neutral and earthy colors.
"I mix **white** and **black** to make gray paint."
🇲🇦 Bied, Khel, Qehwi / 🇫🇷 Blanc, Noir, Marron
Monday, Wednesday, Friday
Days of the week. Capitalize the first letter!
"I attend anthropology lectures on **Monday**."
🇲🇦 Tnin, Larba, Jm3a / 🇫🇷 Lundi, Mercredi, Vendredi
Saturday, Sunday
The weekend. Time to rest and paint!
"On **Sunday**, I watch football."
🇲🇦 Sebt, L7ad / 🇫🇷 Samedi, Dimanche
Years: 1985 / 2026
Read as two parts: nineteen eighty-five / twenty twenty-six.
"I retired in 2021 (twenty twenty-one)."
🇫🇷 Dix-neuf cent quatre-vingt-cinq
💡 Number Tip: Tens (-ty) vs Teens (-teen)
Listen carefully to the endings!
13 = thir**teen** (🇫🇷 treize)
30 = thir**ty** (🇫🇷 trente)
🇲🇦 Tletash vs Tlatin / 🇫🇷 Treize vs Trente

Step 2 — 📖 Mini-Story (Reading)
The Master's Agenda
10 min
Objective: Read about a typical week in Baba Fattah's life and find details.
📋 Teacher Note: Read this out loud. Point out the pronunciation of dates and years like "nineteen eighty-two".

I started working at Barid Bank in **1982** (nineteen eighty-two). I dedicated **39** (thirty-nine) years of my life to the bank before retiring in **2021** (twenty twenty-one).

Now, my week has a peaceful rhythm. On **Monday** and **Wednesday**, I study at Ibn Tofail University. On **Friday** morning, I visit the market to buy fresh vegetables.

On the weekend, I enter my art room. I paint Mehdya's sunset using bright **red**, warm **yellow**, and deep **blue** colors. I am very happy with my retired life.

📝 Check Your Understanding

1. In what year did Fattah start working at the bank?

2. On which days does he study at the university?

3. What colors does he use to paint Mehdya's sunset?

Step 3 — 🏗️ Vocabulary in Action
Writing Years & Dates
10 min
Objective: Practice writing numbers and years out in English words.
📐 Year Rules:
Years before 2000 → Split in half: 1994 = **nineteen** (19) **ninety-four** (94)
Years after 2000 → Say "two thousand and..." or split: 2026 = **twenty twenty-six**
