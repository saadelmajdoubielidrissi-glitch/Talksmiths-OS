04
Roleplay
At Rimi Supermarket — Real Shopping 🛒
10 MIN
🙋 Sina (You) (to store employee):
Vabandage! Kus on kanafilee ja kohupiim? (Excuse me! Where are the chicken breast and cottage cheese?)
🧑 Töötaja (Employee):
Kana on lihaleti juures, ja kohupiim on seal, piimatoodete riiulil. (Chicken is at the meat counter, and cottage cheese is there, on the dairy shelf.)
🙋 Sina (You) (at checkout):
Tere! Maksan kaardiga, palun. (Hello! I'll pay by card, please.)
💳 Kassapidaja (Cashier):
Palun sisestage PIN. Kaheksateist eurot viiskümmend senti. (Please enter PIN. €18.50.)
🙋 Sina (You):
Aitäh! Head päeva! (Thank you! Have a good day!)
💳 Kassapidaja:
Palun! Nägemist! (You're welcome! Goodbye!)
💡 Rimi, Maxima, and Prisma are the main supermarket chains in Estonia. Rimi and Maxima are the most common in Tartu. They have a "Rimi+" loyalty card which you should get as a student — it saves money!
