03
Speaking Test
The Full A1 Oral Assessment — 5 Scenarios 🎤
25 MIN
This is your final speaking test. For each scenario, speak for 60 seconds minimum without stopping. Record yourself if possible. Write notes before speaking.
Scenario 1 — Meeting someone new (2 min)

You just met Mart at a networking event in Tallinn. Introduce yourself fully, ask about him, respond to his questions. Use at least 8 full sentences.

Scenario 2 — At the pharmacy (1.5 min)

You have a bad cold: sore throat, fever, and headache. Go to Benu Apteek and get what you need. Write the full dialogue.

Scenario 3 — Asking for directions (1.5 min)

You're near Viru Gate and need to reach Telliskivi Creative City (Telliskivi Loomelinnak). Write the dialogue where you ask for and receive directions.

Scenario 4 — Planning a weekend (2 min)

Invite your Estonian colleague Tiina to do something on Saturday. Suggest a time, place, and activity. She has a counter-proposal. Negotiate and confirm.

Scenario 5 — Describing your life in Estonia (2 min)

Describe your daily life in Tallinn: where you live, your work, what you eat, how you get around, and what you do for fun. Use 10+ sentences.

Next Steps
Your A2 Roadmap — What Comes Next 🗺️
Reference
A2
A2 Level — Months 4–6
Past tense (minevik), future tense, all 14 noun cases, more complex sentence structures. You can describe past events, give opinions, and have real conversations.
B1
B1 Level — Months 7–12
Complex grammar, reading Estonian news, watching Estonian TV without subtitles, having conversations about opinions and current events. You can pass the B1 citizenship language test.
🏛️
Estonian Language Exam (B1)
Required for permanent residency and citizenship. Organised by Innove. Tests reading, writing, listening, and speaking. Register at innove.ee.
