Mul on kõhuvalu. — I have a stomachache. (kõht + valu)
Mul on hambavalu. — I have a toothache. (hammas + valu)
Mul valutab selg. — My back hurts. (valutab = hurts)

04
Roleplay
At Benu Apteek — Pharmacy Visit 💊
10 MIN
🙋 Sina (You):
Tere! Mul on peavalu ja köha. Kas teil on valuvaigistit? (Hello! I have a headache and a cough. Do you have a painkiller?)
👩‍⚕️ Apteeker (Pharmacist, Merike):
Jah, meil on spetsiaalne spordikreem ja ibuprofeen. Kas teil on retsept? (Yes, we have a special sports cream and ibuprofen. Do you have a prescription?)
🙋 Sina (You):
Ei, mul ei ole retsepti. Kreem ja ibuprofeen sobivad. (No, I don't have a prescription. The cream and ibuprofen are fine.)
👩‍⚕️ Merike:
See on ilma retseptita. Üks pakk maksab neli eurot. Võtke üks tablett iga kuue tunni järel. (This is without prescription. One pack costs €4. Take one tablet every 6 hours.)
🙋 Sina (You):
Aitäh! Väga lahke. (Thank you! Very kind.)
💡 Key phrase for any medicine: "Kuidas seda ravimit võtta?" (How do I take this medicine?) Memorise this one — it's critical.
