The Estonian Alphabet — The Õ Challenge 🔤
12 MIN
Estonian uses the Latin alphabet with 6 special characters. The unique letter Õ has no equivalent in most European languages — it's Estonia's national sound.

Estonian has 23 letters. The special ones: Ä · Ö · Ü · Õ · Š · Ž

LetterSounds likeExample wordMeaning
Ä ä"ae" as in "cat"äribusiness
Ö ö"ur" as in French "feu"tööwork
Ü ü"ew" as in French "tu"üksone

04
Vocabulary
Introducing Yourself — Minu nimi on ___ 🙋
10 MIN
The first real conversation you'll have in Estonia. These structures come from Keeleklikk Module 1 and Loecsen "Basics 1".
Minu nimi on ___
My name is ___
Structure: Minu nimi on + [your name]
🔊 MI-noo NI-mi on ...
Ma olen ___st
I am from ___ (your country)
-st suffix = "from". Hispaania → Hispaaniast, Saksamaa → Saksamaalt
🔊 Mah OH-len [country]-st
Ma elan Tartus
I live in Tartu
-s suffix = "in a place". Tallinn → Tallinnas, Pärnu → Pärnus
🔊 Mah EH-lan TAR-toos
Mul on ... aastat
I am ... years old
e.g. Mul on kolmkümmend kaks aastat (I'm 32)
🔊 Mool on ... AAS-tat
Ma olen tudeng / üliõpilane
I am a student / university student
tudeng = student (casual), üliõpilane = university student (formal)
🔊 Mah OH-len TOO-deng
Mis teie nimi on?
What is your name? (formal)
Informal: Mis sinu nimi on?
🔊 Mis TEI-eh NI-mi on
🇪🇪 Grammar Bridge — The Case Suffixes (-st · -s)
Estonian nouns change ending depending on their role in the sentence — this is called case declension. Today's two cases:
Elative (-st): "from" → Hispaaniast, Saksamaalt, Eestist
Inessive (-s): "in" → Tartus, Tallinnas, Pärnus
Deep dive: Keeleklikk Module 3 covers all 14 Estonian cases. Don't panic — you'll learn them one by one.

05
Roleplay
First Meeting in Tartu — Raekoja Plats 🏙️
8 MIN
Read the dialogue aloud 3 times: slowly, then at normal speed, then from memory. Mart is your new Estonian neighbour.
🧑 Mart (Neighbour):
Tere! Minu nimi on Mart. Mis teie nimi on?
(Hello! My name is Mart. What is your name?)
🙋 Sina (You):
Tere! Minu nimi on ___. Ma olen ___st. Ma elan nüüd Tartus. Olen Erasmuse tudeng.
(Hello! My name is ___. I'm from ___. I now live in Tartu. I am an Erasmus student.)
🧑 Mart:
Ahaa! Tere tulemast Eestisse! Kas te õpite Tartu Ülikoolis?
(Oh! Welcome to Estonia! Do you study at the University of Tartu?)
🙋 Sina (You):
Jah, ma olen Erasmuse tudeng. Ma õpin ülikoolis ja käin ka spordiklubis!
(Yes, I am an Erasmus student. I study at the university and I also go to the sports club!)
🧑 Mart:
Tore! Sa õpid eesti keelt kiiresti! Head aega!
(Nice! You'll learn Estonian fast! Farewell!)
🙋 Sina (You):
Nägemist, Mart! Aitäh!
(Goodbye, Mart! Thank you!)
