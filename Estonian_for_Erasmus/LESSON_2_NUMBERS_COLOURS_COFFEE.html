04
Roleplay
Kohvik — Ordering at a Tallinn Café ☕
10 MIN
From Konsta.app "Ordering Coffee" scenario. This is a real survival conversation you'll need on Week 1 in Estonia.
☕ Barrista (Liis):
Tere! Mida soovite? (Hello! What would you like?)
🙋 Sina (You):
Tere! Üks kohv, palun. Kui palju maksab? (Hello! One coffee, please. How much does it cost?)
☕ Liis:
Kaks eurot viiskümmend senti. (€2.50)
🙋 Sina (You):
Palun. Aitäh! (Here you go. Thank you!)
☕ Liis:
Palun! Head isu! (You're welcome! Enjoy!)
💡 "Kui palju maksab?" (How much does it cost?) is one of the most useful A1 phrases. "Kui palju maksab see?" = "How much does THIS cost?" (pointing). Practice this until it's automatic.

05
Vocabulary
Shopping Phrases — Poes ostlemine 🛒
10 MIN
You will need these phrases in supermarkets, pharmacies, and markets within your first week in Tallinn.
Kui palju maksab?
How much does it cost?
Kui palju maksab see? (How much is this?)
🔊 KOO-ee PAL-yoo MAK-sab
Kus asub...?
Where is...?
Kus asub kassа? (Where is the checkout?)
🔊 Koos AH-soob
Kas teil on...?
Do you have...?
Kas teil on piim? (Do you have milk?)
🔊 Kas tail on
Ma tahan...
I want...
Ma tahan leiba. (I want bread.)
🔊 Mah TAH-han
Arve, palun
The bill / receipt, please
Used in restaurants and cafés
🔊 AR-veh PAH-loon
Kaardiga / Sularahas
By card / In cash
Maksan kaardiga. (I'll pay by card.)
🔊 KAAR-di-gah / SOO-lah-rah-has
